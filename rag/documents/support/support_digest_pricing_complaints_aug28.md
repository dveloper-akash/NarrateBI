# Support Digest: Premium Pricing Customer Feedback — Week of Aug 22–28, 2026

- **Date / Time**: `2026-08-28 18:00:00 UTC`
- **Service**: `cart-service`, `pricing-engine`
- **Severity**: SEV-3 (Customer Experience)
- **Event Type**: support_digest
- **Status**: Monitoring

## Summary

Customer support received an elevated volume of pricing-related complaints in the week of Aug 22–28, following the pricing-engine v3.1.0 deployment. Total tickets: 348 (vs. 112 the prior week, +211%).

## Top Complaint Categories

| Category                         | Tickets | % of Total |
|----------------------------------|---------|------------|
| "Price too high / sticker shock" | 189     | 54%        |
| "Discount code not working"      | 97      | 28%        |
| "Cart abandoned — reconsidering" | 41      | 12%        |
| Other                            | 21      | 6%         |

## Qualitative Themes

- Many returning customers cite that previously applied promotional codes (e.g. SUMMER20, LOYAL15) are now invalid at checkout
- Electronics price increase perceived as abrupt (no customer communication)
- Mobile checkout abandonment rate particularly elevated (abandonment at final payment step)

## Agent Actions

- No refunds/price overrides authorized per commercial policy
- Standard response template deployed for discount code queries
- Escalated to commercial analytics team for business decision review

## Business Signal

The spike in "discount code not working" complaints directly correlates with the discount suppression policy activated in pricing-engine v3.1.0 targeting New and Returning segments. Customer lifetime value risk is flagged for affected cohort.
