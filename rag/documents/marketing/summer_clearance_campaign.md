- Attribution model counts only completed conversions — customers who abandoned due to price shock are NOT captured in ROAS denominator

## Contradiction Note

Marketing dashboards report strong ROAS (+35%) for the same week that the revenue ledger shows a -12% decline. This is NOT a discrepancy — it arises from measurement population differences:

- **ROAS**: computed on conversions that DID complete (high-intent, Premium/Enterprise segment buyers unaffected by discount suppression)
- **Revenue decline**: driven by the volume of sessions that DID NOT convert (New/Returning segments deterred by price increases and disabled discount codes)

Both metrics are accurate. They measure different sub-populations.

## Recommendation

Marketing should NOT adjust bids based on ROAS alone during the pricing experiment window. Total revenue is the primary business health signal.
